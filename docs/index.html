Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Buy Here Pay Here car financing?

Buy Here Pay Here means the dealership finances the vehicle directly instead of sending your application to an outside bank. You buy the car and make your payments at the same place — which usually means faster decisions and more flexibility for buyers with limited or damaged credit.

Do I need good credit to get approved?

No. Our financing is built for buyers with bad credit or no credit history at all. We look at your current income and ability to make payments rather than relying solely on a credit score.

What documents do I need to apply?

Generally, you'll need a valid government-issued ID, proof of income, and proof of residence. Call us at 848-304-4842 and our team will confirm exactly what to bring for your situation.

Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Yes. Bring your current vehicle in and we'll evaluate it for trade-in value, which can be applied toward your down payment on a car from our lot.

How do I make my payments?

Since financing is handled in-house, payments are made directly to us on a schedule we set together when you sign — with the plan built around your pay schedule, not a rigid bank calendar.
